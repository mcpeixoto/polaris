# Trademark policy

The Polaris **code** is free software under AGPL-3.0-only. The Polaris **name and logo**
are not, and this page explains the difference.

The separation is deliberate and standard. Anyone may fork the code, modify it, and run it;
what nobody may do is publish that fork under this name, because then "Polaris" stops
telling a user anything about what they are installing — and the first time a modified
build ships a security regression under our name, both the users and the project lose.

## What you may do without asking

- Run Polaris, modified or not, for any purpose.
- Fork it and distribute your fork under **a different name**.
- Say your product "is built on Polaris", "works with Polaris", or "is a fork of Polaris",
  as long as it is not presented as being Polaris or endorsed by us.
- Use the name in articles, talks, comparisons, tutorials and reviews. No permission is
  needed to write about a thing.
- Use the logo unmodified to link to this project.

## What needs permission

- Naming your product, service, company or domain "Polaris", or something close enough to
  be confused with it.
- Using the logo as your own, or modifying it.
- Offering a hosted service called "Polaris", or one whose branding implies it is ours.
- Implying endorsement, affiliation or partnership.

## If you distribute a modified build

Change the name and the logo. Keeping ours on a build we did not make means our security
advisories do not describe your software, and your users have no way to know that.
